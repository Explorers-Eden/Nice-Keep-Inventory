## 📖 Description
**Nice Keep Inventory** changes how the `keep_inventory` game rule works: when you die, you only keep part of your inventory. Important items like armor and tools stay with you, which makes for a fair compromise between losing all your stuff and keeping everything.

On top of that, the data pack comes with customizable **Player Graves** and three new enchantments: **Safekeep**, **Deathlock** and **Gravehold**.

<br>

## 🔎 Content Overview (Wiki)
Want to know which items you keep or how the graves and enchantments work? It's all in our **[Wiki](https://wiki.explorerseden.eu/en/nice_keep_inventory/home)**:
- 💀 **Death Penalties**: partial item loss, player head drops, experience loss and equipment damage, all adjustable
- 🪦 **Graves**: your dropped items are stored safely at the last safe spot before you died
- ✨ **Enchantments**: Safekeep (keep everything at the cost of durability), Deathlock (only you can loot your items) and Gravehold (your items last twice as long)
- ⚙️ **Administration**: an in-game config menu (`/function keep_inventory`), including restoring a player's inventory

## 🛠️ Technical Gibberish
This data pack also works as the **required** resource pack for its translations. Just put the same file into your `resourcepacks` folder and enable it in game.

_(Not needed for the auto-converted mod versions in Single-Player.)_

**PaperMC users:** set the `paper.maxChatCommandInputSize` property to a value between 512 and 1024 (see the [PaperMC Docs](https://docs.papermc.io/paper/reference/system-properties/#papermaxchatcommandinputsize)).
